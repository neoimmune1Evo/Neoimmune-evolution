9. Closed-Loop Adaptation

1. Predict: Map the simulated tumor and rank fictional target candidates.
2. Challenge: Use Red-Team escape events to stress-test the strategy.
3. Validate: In real research, test target biology, immune effects, normal-cell safety, RNA delivery, and preclinical evidence.
4. Monitor: A future research workflow might use clinically appropriate imaging, molecular testing, and ctDNA context.
5. Adapt: Update the Digital Twin, re-check coverage/risk/escape, and run the loop again.

10. Safety and Evidence Gate

🔬 Confirm tumor mutation and RNA expression.
🧪 Investigate HLA presentation and immune recognition where relevant.
🛡️ Test normal-cell and normal-tissue safety.
🚚 Validate RNA loading, protection, uptake, release, and toxicity.
🐁 Obtain appropriate preclinical evidence.
👩‍⚕️ Require clinical, ethical, regulatory, and consent review.
AI cannot pass this gate alone. Simulation results are research hypotheses only and cannot establish safety or clinical efficacy.
