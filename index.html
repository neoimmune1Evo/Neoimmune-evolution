6. Simulated Tumor Evolution Timeline

Clone proportions are shown over ten simulated time points. The values are generated from transparent demonstration rules, not real clinical probabilities.

7. Modeled Evolution Pathways

The network shows baseline clones, selected strategy, activated Red-Team events, possible escape, and the final dominant modeled clone.

8. Closed-Loop Monitoring

NEOIMMUNE AI treats treatment as a repeating process rather than a one-time prediction.

1. Baseline tumor and normal-tissue information are mapped.
2. Candidate targets and safety risks are prioritized.
3. The Red Team tests modeled escape routes.
4. Laboratory and preclinical validation are required.
5. Follow-up data may include clinical assessment, imaging, molecular testing, or ctDNA where appropriate.
6. If recurrence or molecular evolution is suspected, the Digital Twin is updated and the framework is repeated.

9. Safety and Evidence Gate

Passing a computer simulation does not establish safety or clinical efficacy.

🔬 Target presence and expression must be confirmed.
🧪 Immune recognition must be investigated when relevant.
🛡️ Appropriate normal-cell safety testing is required.
🚚 RNA cargo and nanocarrier delivery require validation.
🐁 Appropriate preclinical studies are required.
👩‍⚕️ Clinical, ethical, regulatory, and patient-consent processes are required.
