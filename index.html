9. Closed-Loop Adaptation

1. Predict: Map simulated clones and rank candidate targets.
2. Challenge: Activate possible escape mechanisms through the Red Team.
3. Validate: In real research, test target biology, immune effects, normal-cell safety, RNA delivery, and preclinical performance.
4. Monitor: Future research may use clinically appropriate imaging, molecular testing, and ctDNA context.
5. Adapt: Update the Digital Twin, re-check targets and risks, then run the loop again.

10. Safety and Evidence Gate

🔬 Target mutation and RNA expression confirmation.
🧪 HLA presentation and immune-recognition testing where relevant.
🛡️ Normal-cell and normal-tissue safety testing.
🚚 RNA and nanocarrier loading, uptake, release, and toxicity testing.
🐁 Appropriate preclinical evidence.
👩‍⚕️ Clinical, ethical, regulatory, and patient-consent review.
AI cannot pass this gate alone. A simulation is only a research hypothesis, not evidence of safety or clinical efficacy.
