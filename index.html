8. Closed-Loop Monitoring

NEOIMMUNE AI treats treatment as a repeated learning and validation process, not a one-time prediction.

1. Map baseline tumor and normal-tissue information.
2. Rank candidate targets and potential safety risks.
3. Use the Red Team to test possible escape routes.
4. Require laboratory and preclinical validation.
5. Monitor through clinically appropriate assessment, imaging, molecular testing, or ctDNA where appropriate.
6. Update the Digital Twin if recurrence, resistance, or evolution is suspected.

9. Safety and Evidence Gate

A computer result is not sufficient evidence for patient treatment.

🔬 Confirm target presence and expression.
🧪 Investigate immune recognition where relevant.
🛡️ Test appropriate normal-cell safety.
🚚 Validate RNA cargo and nanocarrier delivery.
🐁 Conduct appropriate preclinical studies.
👩‍⚕️ Require clinical, ethical, regulatory, and consent review.

Suggested Presentation Statement

“NEOIMMUNE AI Lab is a cancer-type–agnostic research visualization prototype. A user can select a cancer context, load a simulated heterogeneous tumor model, test target combinations, activate plausible cancer-escape mechanisms, and visualize possible tumor-evolution pathways under defined assumptions. It is intended for research education and hypothesis generation, not patient-specific treatment recommendation.”
