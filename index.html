How the Framework Works

Map Rank Filter Design Challenge Validate Monitor Adapt

The framework maps simulated tumor heterogeneity, ranks fictional target candidates, filters normal-tissue risk, designs multi-target strategies, challenges them with Red Team scenarios, and updates the Digital Twin.

Evidence gate

  • Mutation and RNA confirmation.
  • HLA presentation and immune-recognition studies.
  • Normal-tissue safety testing.
  • RNA and nanocarrier delivery studies.
  • Preclinical evidence.
  • Clinical, ethical, regulatory, and consent review.
AI cannot pass this gate alone. The website produces educational simulation outputs, not clinical recommendations.
