3. AI-2: Neoantigen Evaluator and Safety Filter

CANDIDATE PRIORITIZATION — REQUIRES VALIDATION

A real neoantigen workflow would compare tumor DNA with matched normal DNA, identify protein-changing somatic mutations, confirm RNA expression, consider HLA presentation, assess clone coverage, compare normal-tissue expression, and test immune recognition.

Candidate Mutation evidence RNA evidence HLA context Clone coverage Normal risk Status
Conceptual priority score:
Sᵢ = wₘMᵢ + wₑEᵢ + wₕHᵢ + w𝚌Cᵢ + wₚPᵢ − wᵣRᵢ − wᵤUᵢ
M = mutation evidence, E = RNA expression, H = HLA-presentation evidence, C = clone coverage, P = predicted immunogenicity, R = normal-tissue risk, U = uncertainty. This is only a conceptual, non-clinically validated scoring model.
WHY VALIDATION IS REQUIRED Predicted neoantigens may not be expressed, presented by HLA, recognized by immune cells, stable across tumor clones, or safe for normal tissues.

9. AI-6: Monitoring and Digital Twin Update

CLOSED-LOOP RESEARCH WORKFLOW
Baseline: Map currently available tumor, normal-tissue, and patient-context information.
Candidate design: Rank targets, evaluate predicted safety, and create a research candidate strategy.
Challenge: Run the Therapeutic Red Team to test modeled escape routes.
Validate: Confirm targets, immune effects, normal-cell safety, RNA delivery, and preclinical activity.
Monitor: Use clinically appropriate assessment, imaging, ctDNA/liquid biopsy when appropriate, and molecular testing when indicated.
Update: If recurrence, resistance, or molecular change is suspected, update the Digital Twin and repeat the loop.
CLOSED-LOOP PRINCIPLE Predict → challenge → validate → review → treat under appropriate supervision → monitor → detect change → update → adapt → validate again.

10. Safety and Evidence Gate

AI CANNOT PASS THIS GATE ALONE
🔬 Target presence and RNA expression require experimental confirmation.
🧪 HLA presentation and immune recognition require investigation where relevant.
🛡️ Normal-tissue and normal-cell risk must be assessed.
🚚 RNA loading, protection, uptake, release, and toxicity require validation.
🐁 Appropriate preclinical evidence is required before human evaluation.
👩‍⚕️ Clinical experts, ethics review, regulatory requirements, and informed consent are essential.

How to Explain This Website

“NEOIMMUNE AI Lab is an educational, hypothesis-generation prototype. It demonstrates how a cancer research framework could combine tumor heterogeneity mapping, neoantigen prioritization, normal-tissue safety evaluation, RNA and nanocarrier feasibility, therapeutic Red-Team testing, immune-escape simulation, validation gates, and longitudinal monitoring. The output is not a clinical decision; it is a transparent simulated scenario that requires independent laboratory, clinical, ethical, and regulatory evaluation.”
